    Teaching resources

    10 projects
    Mass spectrometry↗

    TIMS 3D

    Explodable, physics-based 3D models of thermal ionization mass spectrometers (Isotopx Phoenix, Nu TIMS, Thermo Triton XT), with traced ion optics, exploded resistor and capacitor amplifier workbenches, step-through Daly and SEM signal chains, Faraday noise, source tuning and total evaporation.

    JavaScript · three.js · Geochronology     Mass spectrometry↗

    LA-ICP-MS 3D

    Explodable 3D models of Thermo iCAP RQ and TQ ICP-MS coupled to 193 nm excimer and femtosecond laser-ablation systems, with quadrupole stability, collision/reaction-cell chemistry, crater growth and washout, and a U–Pb zircon spot workflow.

    JavaScript · three.js · Laser ablation     Mass spectrometry↗

    LA-ICP-ToF-MS 3D

    An explodable Nu Vitesse time-of-flight mass spectrometer, with timed ion packets, reflectron energy focusing, argon blanking, MagneTOF signal formation and the laser-to-pixel acquisition clocks. Includes the accompanying LA-ICP-ToF-MS study notes.

    JavaScript · three.js · Time-of-flight MS     Mass spectrometry↗

    Nu Sapphire 3D

    Explodable 3D models of the Nu Sapphire dual-path MC-ICP-MS, with the collision cell, ESA and magnet double focusing, multicollection and mass bias, and independent low- and high-mass zones for static U–Pb collection.

    JavaScript · three.js · MC-ICP-MS     Isotope geochemistry↗

    Column Chem 3D

    Step-by-step 3D illustrations of chemical abrasion and ion-exchange column chemistry, with simulated element bands for U–Pb anion columns, resin partitioning curves, and a protocol atlas across isotope systems.

    JavaScript · three.js · Clean-lab chemistry     Rock magnetism↗

    Automated Rock Magnetometer 3D

    Explorable 3D models of an automated superconducting rock magnetometer, with a 100-specimen X–Y stage or 200-specimen snake chain, orthogonal pickup coils and SQUID signal electronics.

    JavaScript · three.js · Automated magnetometry     Rock magnetism↗

    VSM 3D

    An explodable vibrating-sample magnetometer, with an electromagnet, vibrating sample rod, differential pickup coils, a voltage preamplifier and phase-sensitive lock-in detection.

    JavaScript · three.js · Inductive magnetometry     Rock magnetism↗

    SQUID Magnetometer 3D

    Explore a superconducting magnetometer from its cryostat and axial gradiometer to the SQUID sensor, Josephson junctions and flux-locked-loop readout, with component zoom-ins and exploded views.

    JavaScript · three.js · Superconducting magnetometry     Paleomagnetism↗

    Sun Compass 3D

    An animated, 3Blue1Brown-style 3D lesson on how a sun compass orients field samples, from hour angle and declination to the astronomical triangle, the shadow angle, and why the azimuth needs atan2 rather than arcsin.

    JavaScript · three.js · Field orientation     Learning resources↗
