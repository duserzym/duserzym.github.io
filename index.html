    Paleomagnetism · Rock magnetism · Geochronology

    Yiming Zhang

    I integrate paleomagnetism, rock magnetism, U–Pb geochronology, and thermochronology to reconstruct ancient magmatism, paleogeography, and the evolution of Earth’s magnetic field and interior. The work combines field observations, laboratory measurements, and numerical models.
